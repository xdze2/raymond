## LIST 3: DECLARED & INTENTIONAL DATA

Data produced because someone decided to produce it.
A human or institution as deliberate author.
Subdivide into:

### 3a. ADMINISTRATIVE & LEGAL (~20 items)

Performative records — they do something by existing.
Contracts, certificates, filings, registrations, verdicts.

### 3b. SCIENTIFIC & RESEARCH (~20 items)

Produced to establish truth claims.
Studies, trials, observations, measurements with intent.
Flag unpublished or suppressed examples with [MISSING].

### 3c. CORPORATE & INSTITUTIONAL (~20 items)

Produced by organizations about themselves or their activities.
Filings, reports, disclosures, internal records.
Flag items that exist but are not public with [BURIED].

### 3d. JOURNALISTIC & INVESTIGATIVE (~15 items)

Produced to reveal, expose, or document.
Include landmark datasets that came from leaks or investigations.

## LIST 4: DERIVED & COMPUTED DATA

Data that didn't exist anywhere — produced by transforming other data.
Algorithm or methodology as author.
Subdivide into:

### 4a. STATISTICAL AGGREGATES (~15 items)

Constructed from raw data through methodology.
Indices, rates, averages, distributions.
Note: the methodology choice is itself a power decision.

### 4b. INFERRED & PREDICTED (~15 items)

Computed from data to predict or classify something not directly observed.
Scores, risk ratings, recommendations, classifications.
Include who produces it and who is affected.

### 4c. SYNTHESIZED & MODELED (~15 items)

Complex transformations — models, simulations, trained systems.
Climate models, economic forecasts, AI training datasets.
