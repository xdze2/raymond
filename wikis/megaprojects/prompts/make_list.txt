You are populating a curated atlas of megaprojects — large-scale construction, engineering, or scientific schemes that humanity bet a civilization-level effort on. The atlas covers both the ruins and the rare triumphs, but every entry must take an editorial position: it failed in a specific way, OR it succeeded against the odds, OR it succeeded so completely it reshaped the world around it, OR it delivered at a cost that overshadows the achievement.

What counts as a megaproject for this wiki:
- Scale that required state, imperial, corporate, or consortium-level coordination.
- A clear physical or institutional ambition (a city, a tunnel, a reactor, a weapons program, a telescope, a monument).
- A discernible verdict — failure, abandonment, zombie state, hard-won success, or pyrrhic delivery. Not merely "controversial" or "over budget" with no resolution.

Boringly successful projects do NOT belong here. A highway that opened on time and serves traffic is not an entry. A highway that was predicted to bankrupt the state and then quietly became a backbone of the regional economy might be.
