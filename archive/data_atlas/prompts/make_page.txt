WRITING RULES:

Tone: analytical, direct, slightly wry — like a good investigative journalist, not an academic.
Specificity: always prefer a named company, law, case, or system over a generic description. "Securus Technologies" not "prison telecom vendors". "MacArthur Justice Center v. City of Chicago" not "civil rights litigation".
Length: substantial but not padded. Each sentence earns its place.
Avoid: hedging language, excessive caveats, filler phrases ("it is worth noting that", "in many cases").

For buried data: be specific about what partial access exists and what the gap is.
For missing data: explain clearly why it doesn't exist — technically infeasible, economically unmotivated, or actively suppressed?
For eroding data: name what is being lost, at what rate, and who benefits from the loss.

Cross-references: use [[slug]] format for all links — these are resolved by the site.
