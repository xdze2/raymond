Proposed Classification Axes

1. HOW — Primary Innovation Type
   What's the actual lever of change?

new_vehicle — a new physical form factor (Ami, Microlino, FT-Me)
new_propulsion — electrification of existing forms (e-scooter, e-bike)
new_usage_model — shared/free-floating, subscription, rental (Lime, Voi)
regulatory_push — the innovation IS the rule change (ZFE, speed limits, permit reform)
infrastructure — bike lanes, charging hubs, intermodal hubs
software/platform — apps, fleet management, data analytics (Fluctuo)

2. WHY — Primary Driver
   What problem or desire is it solving? (can be multi-tagged)

decarbonation — reducing CO₂ / meeting climate targets
congestion — fewer cars, less traffic
cost — cheaper than owning/operating a car
access — usable without a full licence (age 14+, disabled users)
last_mile — bridging transit and destination
fun/lifestyle — Buggy, Microlino, La Bagnole lean heavily here
health — active mobility (cycling)
logistics — professional delivery use case

3. WHO — Target User

urban_daily — city commuters
periurban — suburbs, mid-density towns (often underserved)
rural — La Bagnole is interesting here
youth — 14–18, pre-licence age
senior/pmr — reduced mobility (Toyota APM angle)
professional — delivery, trades, services
tourist — seasonal, short-trip

4. SCOPE — Actor Type

startup — Kilow, early Lime, Dott
scale_up — Lime, Voi (post-Series B, operating at scale)
legacy_auto — Toyota, Stellantis, Renault
public/city — Vélib', municipal contracts
ngo/advocacy — Mobilians, Observatoire national
research — academic, think tanks

5. FORM FACTOR — Vehicle Category
   Your research makes this essential because regulatory treatment differs by category:

edpm — e-scooters, hoverboards (trottinettes)
e_bike — pedal-assist, speed pedelecs
cargo_bike — family and professional
quadricycle_l6e — sans permis, 45 km/h
quadricycle_l7e — heavy quad, 80 km/h, B1 permit
moped/scooter — 2-wheel motorized
microvan — cargo quads, last-mile delivery boxes

6. STATUS — Lifecycle

concept — Toyota FT-Me (as of today), some Renault Mobilize
pilot — small production run, testing market (early Kilow)
active_growing — Fiat Topolino, Voi Paris
active_mature — Citroën Ami, Vélib'
struggling — Mobilize Duo, Microlino commercially
abandoned — Bird in France, Renault Twizy

7. GEOGRAPHY — Territorial Fit
   Because solutions don't transfer 1:1 between contexts:

dense_urban — Paris intramuros
mid_city — Lyon, Bordeaux, Rennes
periurban — agglomérations, communes rurbanisées
rural — genuine countryside use
cross_border — EU harmonization plays (regulatory axis)

8. BUSINESS MODEL — How it sustains itself

product_sale — you buy the vehicle
subscription — monthly access (Swapfiets model)
pay_per_use — per minute/trip (Lime, Voi)
b2b_fleet — sold/leased to companies
public_contract — city tender (Paris e-bike contracts)
subsidy_dependent — bonus écologique, aides à l'achat

9. REGULATION POSTURE — Relationship with rules
   This is a genuinely discriminating dimension revealed by the research:

regulation_enabled — exists because rules allow it (L6e category)
regulation_neutral — unaffected either way
regulation_challenging — pushed against existing rules (early dockless scooters)
regulation_shaping — actively lobbying/creating new categories (Mobilians, city pilots)
regulation_killed — Paris rental scooter ban
