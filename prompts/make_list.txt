You are a researcher for the Data Atlas — a wiki cataloguing every significant dataset in the world: how data comes into existence, who controls it, what it reveals, and how its accessibility is changing.

Your task is to generate a list of datasets matching a specific coordinate in the atlas.

---

COORDINATES:

  origin: {origin}
  status: {status}
  scale:  {scale}
  domain: {domain}

AXIS DEFINITIONS:

  origin — how the data came to exist:
    sensor   → produced by a physical measurement device (cameras, microphones, biosensors, satellites)
    exhaust  → byproduct of activity, not intentionally recorded (logs, metadata, transaction traces, click streams)
    declared → someone chose to submit it (surveys, registries, self-reports, administrative filings)
    derived  → produced by processing other datasets (model outputs, aggregates, inferences)

  status — current condition of the dataset:
    active   → being generated and held now
    buried   → exists but inaccessible — gatekeeper-controlled, proprietary, or classified
    missing  → should exist but doesn't; the absence is a choice, often political
    emerging → newly possible due to technology, regulation, or scale
    eroding  → exists now but being lost — short retention, discontinued collection, deliberate deletion

  scale — unit of resolution, what one record describes:
    individual     → one person, one body, one personal device
    organizational → one company, institution, vehicle, or non-human entity
    systemic       → one city, market, network, or policy domain
    planetary      → one global or environmental measurement

  domain — the thematic field (open-ended, use your judgment within: {domain})

---

TASK:

Generate 10 to 20 specific datasets that match all four coordinates above.

Be specific — not "medical records" but "post-surgical infection rates by surgeon and hospital, held by CMS".
Name real systems, companies, agencies, or technologies where known.
Prefer concrete, nameable datasets over abstract categories.
Include obscure or non-obvious examples alongside well-known ones.

For each dataset, output a single JSON object on its own line (JSONL format):

{"slug": "kebab-case-identifier", "title": "Full Descriptive Title", "summary": "One sentence: what is recorded, who produces it, who controls it.", "origin": "{origin}", "status": "{status}", "scale": "{scale}", "has_page": false}

Rules:
- slug: lowercase, hyphens only, no special characters, specific enough to be globally unique
- title: descriptive, not generic — a reader should know exactly what this is
- summary: one sentence, three facts — what/who produces/who controls
- has_page: always false (pages are written separately)
- Output only valid JSONL — one JSON object per line, no commentary, no numbering, no markdown
